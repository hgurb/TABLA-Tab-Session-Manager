Your tabs. Back where they belong.

Group as you browse. Organize a whole window in one click. Keep a local record of your groups for the next restart.

A little setup, a lot less tab clutter.

Pin TABLA, then click its icon for grouping, collapse controls, and saved sessions. Settings, pause, undo, and recovery are also available in the menu.

Settings

Automatic grouping

Same domain

Domain matching uses the registered domain: docs.google.com and mail.google.com match. Separate sites such as alice.github.io and bob.github.io remain separate.

In domain mode, matching domains are needed to start a group. Once a group exists, this option allows links from any domain to join it.

0 = unlimited; otherwise choose 2–999. When an automatic group is full, the new tab stays outside. Grouping splits new sets as needed; existing groups are never split.

Tabs that do not join a group keep the placement chosen by Chrome or their creator. TABLA only removes incompatible inherited group membership; Chrome may place the ungrouped tab at a group boundary. Tabs created inside a group are checked even when no opening-tab information is provided.

Keep the selected tab’s group open and collapse other groups in the focused window. Selecting an ungrouped tab collapses every group. Applies when enabled, when switching tabs or windows, and after startup recovery finishes.

Expanding a group returns to the tab you last used in it. If no previous tab is available, select its first tab. TABLA’s Expand buttons keep your current tab selected.

Automatic grouping follows links opened from a source tab. Typing a new address in an existing tab does not reshuffle your groups. New group colors follow site favicons when available. Manually chosen group names and colors are preserved.

Menu grouping action

Current window only

Merge existing groups with exactly the same nonempty name, keeping the first group’s name and color. Ungrouped tabs follow your selected grouping rules. Existing browser and extension tabs stay inside their groups when merging. Ungrouped browser pages, pinned tabs, incognito windows, and groups containing excluded websites are left alone.

Use a separate grouping mode

Source mode uses known opener relationships, including relationships TABLA recorded earlier. Tabs with unknown origins stay untouched. Right-click the icon to undo the last grouping change in that window.

After the menu’s Group this window action, collapse both new and existing groups in this window, including groups that were not regrouped. This also works when there are no new groups to create.

These actions use your saved settings. Save any changes first.

Persistence

Stored on this device

Saves membership, names, colors, collapsed state, tab order, and known source families as tabs change. Startup recovery waits for Chrome to restore your windows, then repairs matching tabs without reopening pages. Recent snapshots remain available below.

Recovery preserves your saved groups, including groups with mixed domains. It does not rerun your grouping rules. Incognito browsing is never saved.

Custom rules 0 rules

Match a hostname and its subdomains. Ungrouped matching sites share a named group during toolbar grouping, even across domains. Existing groups keep their membership, name, and color. In automatic mode, matching rules let a new tab join its source group. Auto uses the website’s favicon to choose the closest Chrome group color, with a fallback when no readable icon is available. First matching rule wins; exclusions take priority.

Excluded websites 0 websites

Each entry includes its subdomains. Excluded tabs are left alone by automatic and toolbar grouping. Existing saved groups can still be recovered.

Recovery & backups

Repair groups on currently open tabs using a saved layout. Tabs are matched within windows by URL and occurrence. No tabs are opened or closed. Identical windows or duplicate URLs can make recovery ambiguous.
